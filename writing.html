High-level writing checklist

Before you submit, ask yourself:

My response clearly answers the prompt.
The opening creates a reason to keep reading.
Each paragraph has a purpose.
The problem develops rather than appearing suddenly.
My character's feelings are shown through actions and reactions.
The climax matters to the story.
I used precise words instead of unnecessary “big words”.
I varied sentence length and structure.
My ending feels earned, not rushed.
I checked grammar, spelling and punctuation.

Model example: Show, don't tell

The goal is not to hide the emotion completely. Give the reader evidence.

Tell

“Mia was terrified.”

The emotion is named, but the reader gets little evidence.
Show

“Mia's fingers tightened around the torch. Somewhere beyond the trees, a branch snapped.”

Physical reaction + sensory detail lets the reader infer fear.
Selective-level aim

Choose details that also move the scene forward.

Every strong detail should earn its place.
